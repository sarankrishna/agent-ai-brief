## Three signals that should change your mental model

### Inference optimisation is becoming a feedback-engineering discipline
Z.ai reports a production inference workflow in which an Infra Agent proposes changes and layered correctness, tracing, microbenchmark and end-to-end feedback determines what survives. The reported result is a less-than-two-week path to production and roughly 3x throughput on GLM-5.3-Flash. The architectural lesson is stronger than the vendor numbers: useful agentic systems need attributable feedback loops, not only code-generation ability.

Source: [Z.ai, Toward Recursive Self-Improvement](https://z.ai/blog/glm-built-its-inference-infrastructure)

### Agent serving is a heterogeneous scheduler problem
A new study reports task-dependent CPU, disk-I/O and memory bottlenecks in concurrent agents, with CPU-aware admission and allocation improving CPU-sensitive latency by about 5.4x in its evaluation. Model latency alone is therefore an insufficient capacity-planning metric.

Source: [arXiv, Not All AI Agents Are Equal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19947)

### Runtime orchestration is becoming part of the product
GitHub now exposes efficiency, balance and intelligence tiers for model selection, while its HydraFusion research preview selects single, cascade or critique workflows. The benchmark claims are explicitly offline and preview-stage, but the control-plane direction is clear: quality, cost and latency are increasingly managed by policy rather than a fixed model choice.

Sources: [GitHub Copilot releases](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-github-copilot-weekly-releases-september-14), [HydraFusion](https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/project-hydrafusion-frontier-quality-via-multi-model-orchestration/)

### 5. Chronicle proposes replayable agent regression tests
- **Type:** New research submission and implementation.
- **Evidence:** Chronicle records nondeterministic boundaries as immutable envelopes and replays selected boundaries while executing others live. On six simulated failures it reports bit-stable full replay across 20 repetitions and detection of all tested unsafe-action mutants.
- **Maturity:** Research prototype with a small benchmark.
- **Why it matters:** Recorded incidents can become executable CI tests rather than passive traces.
- **Architecture read:** Version model, tool and environment boundaries; support full and cut-point replay while excluding secrets and uncontrolled mutable state.
- **What is not proven:** Generalisation to real providers, larger failure sets and production-scale state.
- **Decision:** Experiment.
- **Source:** [arXiv, Chronicle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20625)

## Architecture patterns emerging

### Feedback-rich agent engineering loops
Evidence from Z.ai and Chronicle points to the same pattern: intermediate behaviour must be observable, attributable and replayable. Implication: traces, local validators and replayable fixtures should be platform primitives. **Architect’s question:** Which boundaries can be replayed without capturing sensitive or mutable state?

### Runtime policies as the optimisation unit
GitHub’s routing workflows, SoL-Pi’s harness mechanisms and task-aware resource allocation all move optimisation above the model call. Implication: deploy and roll back routing, admission and harness policy independently of model versions. **Architect’s question:** Can policy changes be evaluated on quality, cost, latency and failure rate before release?

### Heterogeneous agent infrastructure
AWS/NVIDIA, Huawei and the resource-dynamics study all point beyond GPU-only serving. Implication: inference, tools, memory and orchestration need workload-aware placement and explicit resource contracts. **Architect’s question:** What workload taxonomy is predictive enough for admission and placement?
