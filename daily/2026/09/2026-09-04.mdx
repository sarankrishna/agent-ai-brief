## Architecture patterns emerging

### Effort-aware routing
**Evidence:** Gemini effort levels and iterative tools, plus availability across many agent surfaces.
**Implication:** Make escalation, budgets, and stop conditions explicit in the control plane.
**Architect question:** Can every escalation and token/tool budget be explained?

### Isolation as a normal agent primitive
**Evidence:** Copilot uses per-session worktrees; OpenAI describes isolation and trajectory monitoring for Astra.
**Implication:** Isolated state, provenance, and review should be defaults.
**Architect question:** Which shared resources remain reachable from a private workspace?

**Architect question:** What evidence is required before an open checkpoint is admitted?
