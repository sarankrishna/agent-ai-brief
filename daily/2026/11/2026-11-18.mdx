## Three signals that should change your mental model

### The coding-model market is separating capability from cost
Microsoft launched two coding models tuned for everyday enterprise workloads, explicitly distinguishing routine/repository work from very long-horizon agent work. The practical shift is not a universal best model, but workload-specific routing and measured cost per resolved task. Microsoft reports strong relative benchmark results, but its comparisons are vendor-published and include promotional pricing. [Primary source](https://microsoft.ai/news/introducing-mai-code-2-and-mai-code-2-flash/)

### Agent orchestration is becoming collaborative rather than merely delegated
Perplexity describes a multi-model team that shares evidence and coordinates changes to shared files, beyond the lead-and-worker delegation pattern. The design makes shared state, conflict resolution, and audit trails first-order engineering concerns. Availability is limited to Perplexity Max web subscribers; the announcement does not establish independent quality gains from adding agents. [Primary source](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-computer-multiagent)

### Robotics tooling is collapsing the integration surface, not eliminating physical validation
Google combines a faster embodied model with an Antigravity workflow for hardware setup, simulation, and closed-loop control. This points toward IDE-like tooling for robot application development, but vendor demos and a small task benchmark do not establish robustness across hardware, safety cases, or deployment conditions. [Primary source](https://developers.googleblog.com/gemini-hydrogen-for-robotics/)

## Architecture patterns emerging

1. **Route by task economics, then escalate:** OpenAI and Microsoft both frame model choice around task complexity and cost, while Microsoft explicitly carves out very long-horizon work. Implement routing against measured success, retries, latency, and effective cost, not model labels or headline averages.
2. **Agent coordination requires a shared-state control plane:** Perplexity’s shared findings and coordinated file edits imply provenance, conflict resolution, scoped write permissions, and human review are part of the agent architecture, not optional observability.
3. **Tooling is moving upstream into data and simulation preparation:** AWS packages dataset preparation and Google packages robotics setup/simulation. The common implication is to make generated workflows inspectable and place human/automated validation gates before costly training or physical actuation.
